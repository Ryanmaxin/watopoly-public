# Watopoly

Watopoly was our CS246 final project at the University of Waterloo in Winter
2023. The course gave us the game and its rules; our group built the terminal
version in C++14.

It's a Waterloo-themed Monopoly game for two to six players. You move around a
40-square board, buy buildings, collect tuition, trade, and try to stay out of
bankruptcy. We built the turn handling, game logic, saving and loading, and text
display. The board updates as players move and properties change.

We worked on it as a team of three: [Ryan Maxin](https://github.com/Ryanmaxin),
[Isha Chaudhry](https://github.com/Ishac1), and
[Nandish Patel](https://github.com/nandishofficial). We can't share the source
publicly because of course academic integrity rules, but you can read about how
we put it together:

- [Final design document](https://github.com/Ryanmaxin/watopoly-public/blob/main/design.pdf)
